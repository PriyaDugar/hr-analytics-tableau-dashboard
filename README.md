#  HR Attrition Analytics Dashboard | Tableau

##  Overview

This project presents an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard developed using Tableau to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and departmental patterns.

The dashboard helps identify employee segments with different attrition patterns and provides insights that can support data-driven HR and employee-retention decisions.

##  Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Employees** – Total number of employees in the organization
- **Attrition Count** – Number of employees who have left the organization
- **Attrition Rate** – Percentage of employees who have left
- **Active Employees** – Number of employees currently working
- **Average Age** – Average age of employees

##  Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following visual analyses:

- **Department-wise Attrition** – Compares employee attrition across departments
- **Gender-wise Attrition** – Analyzes attrition patterns by gender
- **Gender & Age-group Attrition** – Examines attrition across different age groups and genders
- **Education Field-wise Attrition** – Compares attrition across educational backgrounds
- **Job Satisfaction Analysis** – Shows employee distribution across satisfaction levels
- **Employee Distribution by Age Group** – Analyzes the overall workforce by age

##  Key Insights

- Employee attrition varies across different departments, indicating differences in workforce retention.
- Attrition patterns differ across gender and age groups, allowing deeper demographic analysis.
- Employees from different education fields show varying levels of attrition.
- Job satisfaction provides an important dimension for understanding employee engagement and potential retention challenges.
- Age-group analysis provides insights into the overall composition of the organization's workforce.
- Interactive filters allow users to explore attrition patterns across departments, gender, job roles, and job satisfaction levels.
